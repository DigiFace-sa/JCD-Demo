## Content findings for the marketing pages

Paraphrased from the [official destination page](https://www.jeddahcentral.com/en/jeddah-central-destination):

- The waterfront destination combines four landmarks, ten attractions and six districts. Its districts connect culture, wellness, entertainment, retail and daily life.
- The public beach is 2.1 km long, with accessible routes, shade and areas for families.
- The Museum occupies the site of a former desalination plant and interprets Jeddah’s industrial history.
- The marina combines waterfront dining and retail with yacht berths. The Oceanarium links exhibits, coral conservation and marine research.
- Sustainability measures include coral restoration, 40% public realm, shading, mobility, smart infrastructure and outdoor cooling. Investment sectors include residential, hospitality, retail, wellness and commercial.

The [official sustainability page](https://www.jeddahcentral.com/en/sustainability) supports the public-realm and environmental-design emphasis. The [official Oceanarium page](https://www.jeddahcentral.com/en/projects/land-marks/oceanarium-and-coral-farm) provides the reef-exhibit interior render.

The local news summaries paraphrase the official stories below. Their local images use the official destination concept renders already listed above; they are **not event photographs** and must not be captioned as photographs of these events.

Jeddah Central presented investment opportunities and sought private-sector partnerships at the forum in Riyadh. Local summary date: `2026-02-09`.
- **Future Real Estate Forum** — [official article](https://www.jeddahcentral.com/news/reff), also available through the [English article route](https://www.jeddahcentral.com/en/news-and-media/news/reff). A fresh retrieval on 3 November 2026 displays **25 January 2026** on both article routes and the newsroom index; the body says the event ran **26–28 January 2026**. Thus `2026-01-26` is the event start date, not the article date in the HTML currently served. Use `2026-01-25` for a publication-date label, or explicitly label `2026-01-26` as the event date. The story concerns Jeddah Central's strategic-partner participation and marina prequalification plans.
- **Ministry of Sport visit** — [official article](https://www.jeddahcentral.com/news/mos-visit), displayed **22 January 2026**, matching the newsroom index. A ministry delegation visited the stadium site to review construction and discuss technical and organisational aspects. Local summary date: `2026-01-22`.
