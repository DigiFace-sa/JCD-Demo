# Official Jeddah Central source assets

Retrieved 3 November 2026 from the official Jeddah Central website for the marketing pages, as requested by the user. No Masar imagery, logo, source code or content is included. All image files are self-hosted for the offline pitch.

The WebP files preserve the original composition and colours. They were downscaled without upscaling and encoded at quality 84; no crop was applied. These are official architectural concept renders, including the aquarium interior, rather than photographs of completed development.

## Images

**39 official renders**, retrieved 2026-11-03 from jeddahcentral.com (home, destination, sustainability, the four landmark pages and the six district pages). The single source of truth is [`assets/official-images.json`](../assets/official-images.json): `npm run images` downloads any missing original into `.cache/official/` (gitignored) and writes, without cropping or upscaling:

- AVIF at 1280 / 1920 / 2560 px wide, plus the native width when it falls between steps (`public/images/jcdc/<id>-<width>.avif`), the primary format;
- one WebP at ≤1920 px (`public/images/jcdc/<id>.webp`), the fallback;
- the typed size table `src/data/officialImages.ts`.

Total on disk: **25.3 MB** for 133 files.

These are architectural concept renders, not photographs of completed development. Interior exhibit renders (Oceanarium) must not be captioned as photographs of a real reef. Curation excluded "coming soon" placeholders, the location map graphic, a render carrying signage text (Coral City), and lower-resolution duplicates of the same view.

Two IDs were upgraded to higher-resolution originals of the same view: `opera-house` (1440 → 4096 px) and `museum` (1440 → 4096 px).

## Content findings for the marketing pages

Paraphrased from the [official destination page](https://www.jeddahcentral.com/en/jeddah-central-destination):

- The waterfront destination combines four landmarks, ten attractions and six districts. Its districts connect culture, wellness, entertainment, retail and daily life.
- The public beach is 2.1 km long, with accessible routes, shade and areas for families.
- The Museum occupies the site of a former desalination plant and interprets Jeddah’s industrial history.
- The marina combines waterfront dining and retail with yacht berths. The Oceanarium links exhibits, coral conservation and marine research.
- Sustainability measures include coral restoration, 40% public realm, shading, mobility, smart infrastructure and outdoor cooling. Investment sectors include residential, hospitality, retail, wellness and commercial.

The [official sustainability page](https://www.jeddahcentral.com/en/sustainability) supports the public-realm and environmental-design emphasis. The [official Oceanarium page](https://www.jeddahcentral.com/en/projects/land-marks/oceanarium-and-coral-farm) provides the reef-exhibit interior render.
